# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Akter welcomes contributors and users of every background and experience level. Everyone participating in the project deserves a respectful environment, free from harassment and discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, and discuss ideas and code without attacking their authors.
- Give constructive feedback, accept corrections, and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information, credentials, or private communications without permission.
- Make room for people who are new to the project and for perspectives different from your own.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention or imagery, trolling, sustained disruption, and personal attacks are unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces, and when someone represents Akter in a public space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to **support@akter.dev**. Include the relevant links or records and what happened; share only the information needed to investigate. Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and share identifying information only when necessary to investigate or respond. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this reporting process.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and restrict participation. They will consider the impact, severity, context, and repeated behavior when deciding on a response, which may include a correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban. Good-faith reports must not lead to retaliation.

Maintainers are responsible for applying this code fairly, including to their own conduct. A person involved in a report should not decide its outcome.
