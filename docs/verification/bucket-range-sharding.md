# Bucket-range Postgres sharding: required evidence

**Status:** required evidence plan for [proposed ADR 0116](../decisions/0116-bucket-range-postgres-sharding.md), not an executed verification report. Physical data sharding and online moves remain unsupported. A green documentation PR, ordinary Postgres conformance or ADR 0067's same-database ranges do not satisfy these gates.

**Responsibility:** define the evidence required before changing the framework, API or hosted support claim.

**Authority:** verification requirements; current [contracts](../contracts/README.md) remain normative until amended with implementation.

**Owner role:** runtime/database and operations/reliability.

**Change policy:** attach reproducible results and limitations here when implementation exists; mark missing capabilities unsupported rather than converting skips into passes.

## Test boundary and evidence record

Use independently provisioned real Postgres primaries for at least two data shards and a separate coordination database, plus independent runner processes using the public command/query/intent/workflow surfaces. Independent database names on one server can establish SQL locality but cannot establish independent primary failure, WAL lineage or capacity. Also exercise coordinator/data colocation through separate clients. PGlite and mocked SQL are not physical-sharding evidence.

Record the exact framework commit, migration histories, Postgres/runtime versions, driver/pool modes, privileges, WAL/replication configuration, topology, map/epochs, seed and commands. Use asymmetric placements and workloads, not two identical halves. Preserve time-correlated fault schedules, observed database locks, gate transitions, move journal/checkpoints, independently derived expected data/obligations and actual results. Redact connection credentials and tenant payloads. Store durable report links under `docs/verification`; CI artifacts without a retained report are not a permanent support record.

Run the relevant [failure matrix](02-failure-matrix.md), [named invariants](invariants.md) and existing unsharded Postgres/PGlite conformance unchanged. Repeat core sharded behavior and process recovery on every claimed runtime; Bun evidence does not certify Node. Provider/network/HA claims need their own multi-host drills. Extend existing harnesses where they own the behavior rather than adding a parallel conformance system.

| Relay capacity and unavailable shards        | Use a fixed first range, claim more than free permits, or count a failed range as empty.                                | Saturate one range and fail another while a third has due work. | Timers, cron and capped jobs                 | Duplicate a copied timer/tick, reset a lease/cap or hold a data transaction across a provider call.                     | Race timer cancel/replace against firing, kill a singleton during its tick and move its bucket with a claim in flight. Race workflow/payload acceptance/removal with starts on different databases and a move. Refuse on a missing shard; incompatible writers stay fenced. Prove canonical/local metadata cannot diverge through independent policy changes. |

## Online move failure schedule

Exercise the ADR's complete failure table with real connection loss and process death, including commit acknowledgments withheld **after** a database-observed commit. For every schedule, record the journal and local gates before/after restart. An unobserved outcome remains unknown; a timeout or lease expiry is not a closure acknowledgment.

Seed the moved range with nonuniform state/events, owned rows, blobs, sender outbox obligations, full receipts/tombstones, scheduled/capped/attempted/cancelled jobs, subscriptions/cursors, workflows/manifests/waits, connections, tenant content and audit records. Inventory the migrated catalog and every registered owned table independently of the copier. Compare counts plus key/value digests, sequence/generation counters and pending obligations through the final source barrier; counts alone cannot detect exchanging or corrupting two rows. Include updates and deletes during bulk copy. Future eligible adoption/cold-tier extensions need their own records and provider evidence, not assumptions from the actor-generation join.

Assert the central invariant across the schedule: once source closure commits, no runtime mutation commits at its retired epoch; target runtime writes begin only after complete validated history and a durable coordinator decision. Successful recovery preserves every acknowledged local commit and its obligations. Observer traces must distinguish legitimate pre-cutover read snapshots from new stale reads.

## Feature, protocol and operational gates

| Gate                                      | Required evidence before claiming it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Versioned read-your-writes/watch protocol | Two independent primaries have intentionally incomparable WAL positions. A numerically larger LSN from the wrong lineage cannot satisfy a read; one busy bucket cannot erase another bucket's token. Test primary-only sharded reads, both sides of cutover, unknown/future lineage, malformed tokens and client negotiation/refusal across TypeScript, React, Python and served transports. Replica support needs additional same-owner/epoch/lineage replay evidence, not ordinary single-database replica tests.                                                                   |
| Fleet                                     | Initially prove startup refuses Fleet declarations in sharded mode. Before lifting that refusal, prove one locally fenced maintainer/feed per database, independent checkpoints, tenant-RLS subscription routing, coordinator-session loss and slot/primary loss. Move a tenant while recomputing; rebuild to the final source image and establish the target feed before new target writes. No duplicated contributions, false readiness or cross-primary LSN comparison. Global/actor-placed sources remain refused.                                                                |
| Joined inspection and operators           | On every shard preserve view versions/catalog, schema-only readers and read-only/RLS guarantees. Actor inspect/export/repair resolves its current owner and audits on that database. Aggregate pagination/fan-out does not count imported/retired copies; a changing map or failed shard yields explicit retry/incomplete state, not `NotFound`, zero or a fabricated global snapshot. Check credentials/secrets are absent from responses.                                                                                                                                           |
| Adopted tables                            | Initially refuse writable adoption with physical sharding/moves and any read-adopted declaration requiring a remote in-turn read. Existing single-database adoption retains global unique/FK/trigger/role behavior. Any later eligibility exception needs separate evidence for every retained constraint, legacy writer, sequence, catalog and never-activated owner row; the generic copy test cannot certify it.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Migrations/upgrade/downgrade              | Race fresh multi-process startup against separate data/coordination histories; inject one shard's DDL failure and restart. Incompatible shards/releases cannot become ready on another shard's success. Stop non-gate-aware alpha runners before enabling sharding. Contracting DDL and metadata acceptance observe all shards; moves serialize with schema changes. Rehearse consolidation and stopped-runner downgrade rather than dropping the map.                                                                                                                                |
| Backup and HA                             | Perform the full stopped-writer manifest backup/restore with deliveries and provider attempts around the boundary; retain every active database and the coordinator. Refuse an incomplete set/clock rollback. Repeat primary/coordinator failover where claimed: acknowledged closures/decisions survive, old primaries are externally fenced and commit-unknown resolves. Independent online PITR/dumps remain unsupported without a separate consistent-backup protocol.                                                                                                            |
| PGlite and unsharded default              | Refuse multi-database/PGlite coordinator/target configurations and moves. Keep one-directory locking, transactional migrations and current unsharded conformance/flight accounting. Multiple files are not a distributed lock test.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Capacity and operator recovery            | Compare a tuned one-primary baseline with several shards at measured distributed bucket load and an intentionally hot indivisible bucket. Report latency/backlog/throughput, gate waits, cross-shard traffic, per-database/total connections, runner memory, coordinator saturation, copy WAL/disk, healthy move pause and degraded recovery time. Failure/overload must not create unbounded local work or a stale-green report. Rehearse operator abort/forward-recovery with clear phase, barrier and remediation signals. No linear-scaling claim or fixed actor-count threshold. |
| Hosted providers                          | Separate private-platform evidence establishes per-database probes/credential handling, networking/locality, aggregate runner caps, multi-host placement, HA/restore and partial-failure diagnostics. A public framework pass does not establish hosted provisioning or availability.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

## Claim checklist

- Accept/amend the identified contracts and protocol; link implemented interfaces, migrations, failure results and operator procedures to this record.
- Attach actual commands/results for independent-primary, separate-coordinator and multi-process schedules, with the exact supported runtime/provider/feature matrix. Missing or skipped targets remain unsupported.
- Keep static multi-database routing, online moves, Fleet, replicas, adoption exceptions, online backup and hosted operation as distinct evidence claims. Passing one does not imply the others.
- Preserve the unsharded baseline and report new costs/restrictions. The initial design deliberately retains permanent internal-delivery tombstones and a cutover pause; do not label either bounded/zero without different proved semantics.

**Executed sharding evidence in this proposal: none.** Documentation/static/package checks validate the proposal's repository integration only.
