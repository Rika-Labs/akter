# Competitive positioning

**Responsibility:** explain product choice without distorting technical tradeoffs.  
**Authority:** product messaging.  
**Owner role:** product/marketing.

Cloudflare Durable Objects optimize for managed object-local state and edge integration. Rivet Actors optimize for self-hostable durable actor infrastructure and actor-local storage. Durable Actors optimizes for actor-controlled mutation over relational business data, SQL observation, durable work, and an Effect-native programming model.

The comparison must state where each product is a better fit. Do not present product intent as benchmark evidence or claim a provider guarantee the provider does not support.
