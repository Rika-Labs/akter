Every example is an actor.

Small, complete apps you can clone and run. Each one shows the contract, the transaction, a failure, and the recovery, not just the happy path.

Featured

Order and payment

Place inserts the lines, emits OrderPlaced, records the total and enqueues Charge in one commit. The charge runs after the commit, with retries and a dead letter. 

yield* turn.rows(orderLines).insert(lines)
yield* turn.emit(OrderPlaced.make({ total }))
yield* turn.enqueue(Charge.make({ amount: total }))
yield* turn.state.set({ total })
